# Requirements

1. When an explicitly attached PDF contains `scannedPages`, the assistant SHALL offer local vision transcription for those pages, including an all-scan PDF, while retaining extractable text and page order for mixed PDFs.
2. The chat webview SHALL render pages with bundled pdf.js and canvas. Its CSP SHALL retain a script nonce; `localResourceRoots` SHALL be limited to `dist/vendor/pdfjs`. No new library download or remote resource SHALL be required.
3. A request SHALL process at most 3 scanned pages from a PDF of at most 10 MB/20 inspected pages. Each rendered JPEG SHALL have a longest edge at most 1600 px, quality 0.80 and decoded size at most 2,000,000 bytes. Each page SHALL have a 90-second render/transcribe timeout. Exceeding a limit SHALL be reported, not silently omitted.
4. Transcription SHALL run only with the currently selected, loaded LM Studio model reported as `type: "vlm"`. An unsupported or unverifiable selection SHALL show a clear error and suggest `Assistant: Select Local Provider and Model`; the extension SHALL not switch or download models automatically.
5. The transcription prompt SHALL request only verbatim page text with Vietnamese diacritics and SHALL forbid solving the exam, adding code or guessing. Empty/error pages SHALL be explicitly marked unreadable. Each transcript SHALL be limited to 10,000 characters and combined PDF context to 40,000 characters.
6. The user SHALL see editable transcription with Xác nhận and Hủy. Only confirmed text SHALL be merged with PDF text and sent to the model. Hủy SHALL send nothing and persist no unconfirmed scan text/images in session history. Cancellable progress SHALL abort in-flight work and prevent late results from being sent.
7. Plain-Node tests with injected renderer/model SHALL cover scanned fixture detection, confirm/edit, cancel, unsupported provider/model, limits, timeout, empty output and in-flight cancellation. `sidebarViews.ts` SHALL remain free of runtime `vscode` imports. Version SHALL be 0.3.4; full `npm test`, local VSIX packaging/installation and installed-version verification SHALL precede implementation review.
